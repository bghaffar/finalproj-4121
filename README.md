Mapping the Meal Gap: Visualizing Food Insecurity Across U.S. States (2019–2022)

Course: ITCS 4121 – Data and Information Visualization
Project Team: Team 5 

Overview: This project visualizes food insecurity trends across the United States using Feeding America’s Map the Meal Gap dataset (2019–2022).
The goal is to explore:

How food insecurity rates have changed over time across states and regions.

Racial and regional disparities in food insecurity.

Counties and states with the highest annual food budget shortfall.

Differences between child, senior, and overall food insecurity.

All visualizations are implemented using D3.js.
